## Tips for Follow-up

1. **Be patient:** Permission requests may take 1-2 weeks for response
2. **Be prepared for:**
   - They may offer API access (like API.Bible has for NLT)
   - They may require a licensing agreement
   - They may suggest using their existing app instead
3. **Alternatives if denied:**
   - Use RVR1960 (already have)
   - Use Reina Valera 1909 (public domain)
   - Use another modern Spanish translation (if available)
